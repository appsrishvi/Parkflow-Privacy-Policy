Privacy Policy for ParkFlow

Application: ParkFlow (Package: com.rishvi.park_flow)
Effective Date: November 7, 2026 | Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Privacy Commitment: ParkFlow operates strictly as an offline-first parking facility management tool. Your operational data, parking records, vehicle plates, passes, and tariff settings are stored locally on your device and are never sold, rented, tracked, or transmitted to third-party advertising servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how ParkFlow ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when parking managers, facility administrators, and operators use our Android mobile application.

By installing and using ParkFlow, you agree to the collection and handling of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The Application is designed around an offline-first architecture. All operational records created within the Application remain stored locally on the host device:

  • Vehicle Registration Information: Vehicle registration plates, category classification (Two-Wheeler, Four-Wheeler, Other), assigned parking floor and zone slot identifiers.
  • Parking Session Data: Timestamps of vehicle entry and exit, elapsed duration, applied tariff calculations, grace period calculations, and checkout completion status.
  • ParkFlow Data: Pass serial numbers, pass category (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Resident, VIP), validity period, and QR payload references.
  • Optional Customer Contact Info: Customer name and phone number provided voluntarily by the operator for recurring passes or parking desk reference.
  • Facility & Layout Configurations: Organization name, parking facility titles, floor levels, zones, slot counts, hourly rates, daily maximum rates, and grace periods.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

ParkFlow requests only the minimal permissions necessary for core parking desk operations:

  • Camera (Optional / Future Features): Used exclusively for scanning vehicle pass QR codes at entry/exit gates. Images or camera streams are processed locally in real time and are never stored or uploaded.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used exclusively to export and save parking receipts, summary documents, or backup archives directly to your device storage.
  • Haptic Feedback / Vibration: Used to provide tactile confirmation when registering vehicle entry or scanning pass badges.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We believe in total operational sovereignty. We do not share, sell, lease, or monetize your parking data with any third party.

  • No third-party advertising networks or ad identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) are bundled into the application.
  • No behavioral tracking or telemetry SDKs collect user habits.
  • All calculations (tariffs, fees, slot allocation) occur directly on the device CPU.

5. Data Security & Storage Integrity

Your data is stored within your device's isolated application sandbox using Android's standard private application storage and SharedPreferences mechanisms. Access to this data is restricted by the operating system solely to the ParkFlow application.

Users are encouraged to maintain device-level security (PIN, pattern, or biometric screen lock) to prevent unauthorized local access to the application.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your records reside on your device, you have complete control over data retention:

  • You can clear individual transactions, cancel passes, or adjust configurations at any time within the application.
  • Clearing the app storage or uninstalling the application from Android Settings will permanently erase all local databases, passes, and configuration profiles from the device.

7. Children's Privacy

ParkFlow is a business utility intended for parking facility managers, desk operators, and commercial establishments. It is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory adjustments. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
